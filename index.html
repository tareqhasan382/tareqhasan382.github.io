About

I'm a Full Stack Engineer focused on building clean, reliable software. On the backend I work primarily with Node.js, NestJS, and Express.js, designing REST APIs that are fast and easy to maintain.

On the frontend I build modern interfaces with React and Next.js using TypeScript, keeping performance and accessibility at the center of every decision. I'm experienced with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis for data modeling and caching, and I use RabbitMQ and WebSockets for real-time, event-driven features. I also work with Python, FastAPI, FFmpeg, and RAG-based AI pipelines to build intelligent services, and I containerize everything with Docker behind Nginx for consistent, reliable deployments.
